Category System/Component Inspection/Maintenance Task Frequency Legislative Reference Actions
Fire Safety Fire Doors Inspect for damage, ensure sealing and certification labels are intact. Quarterly Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005, Article 17
Test self-closing mechanisms and replace faulty components. Quarterly Fire Safety (England) Regulations 2022
Fire Alarm Systems Test fire alarm call points and detectors. Monthly BS 5839-1:2017
Perform a full system inspection and servicing by a qualified engineer. Annually BS 5839-1:2017
Emergency Lighting Conduct emergency lighting functionality tests. Monthly BS 5266-1:2016
Perform a full duration test of emergency lighting systems. Annually BS 5266-1:2016
Smoke Control Systems Inspect and test smoke extraction systems and automatic opening vents (AOVs). Quarterly BS 9999:2017
Sprinkler Systems Check sprinkler heads and ensure system pressure is adequate. Quarterly BS EN 12845:2015
Conduct a full system inspection by a qualified professional. Annually BS EN 12845:2015
Fire-Stopping (Compartmentation) Check fire-stopping materials around service penetrations. Annually Fire Safety (England) Regulations 2022
Intumescent Seals Inspect intumescent materials around doors, risers, and ducts. Annually BS 476 Part 20-24
Escape Route Clearance Ensure corridors and stairwells remain clear of obstructions. Weekly Fire Safety Order 2005 (Article 14)
Waste Storage Fireproofing Inspect bin stores for fire hazards and suppression systems. Monthly BS 9999:2017
Fire Extinguishers Check extinguisher placement, pressure levels, and accessibility. Monthly BS 5306-3:2017
Conduct a professional maintenance check. Annually BS 5306-3:2017
Water Management Drainage Systems Inspect gullies, drains, and sump pumps for blockages. Quarterly Building Regulations 2010, Part H

Legionella Prevention Test water temperatures to prevent Legionella growth. Quarterly Approved Code of Practice L8 (HSE)
Structural & External Cladding & External Wall Systems Conduct visual checks and fire risk assessments on external walls. Annually PAS 9980:2022
Balconies & Walkways Inspect balconies, handrails, and waterproofing. Annually BS 6180:2011
Movement Joints & Expansion Gaps Ensure expansion joints remain free of obstruction and are correctly sealed. Every 5 Years BS 6093:2006
Roof & Gutters Inspect roofing materials for wear or potential leaks. Annually Building Safety Act 2022

Door Entry & Intercoms Test door entry systems and intercoms for functionality. Monthly Building Regulations Part M
Automated Gates & Barriers Ensure gates and barriers comply with force regulations. Bi-annually Supply of Machinery (Safety) Regulations 2008
Common Area Lighting & Access Check lighting, signage, and emergency exits. Monthly Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005, Article 14
